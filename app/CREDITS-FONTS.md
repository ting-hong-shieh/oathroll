# Self-hosted fonts

Oathroll uses exactly two font families, served locally through `next/font/local`.

- **Fraunces**, variable upright face, by Undercase Type (Phaedra Charles and Flavia Zimbardi). Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/fraunces. Font: `public/fonts/fraunces.ttf`. License: [SIL Open Font License 1.1](public/fonts/OFL-Fraunces.txt).
- **IBM Plex Mono**, Regular, by IBM. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/ibmplexmono. Font: `public/fonts/ibm-plex-mono.ttf`. License: [SIL Open Font License 1.1](public/fonts/OFL-IBM-Plex-Mono.txt).

Downloaded 2026-11-07 from the Google Fonts repository. Original font binaries are unmodified. Display italics are browser-synthesized from Fraunces upright; no third font family or external font service is used. Full copyright notices and OFL texts are included alongside both fonts.
